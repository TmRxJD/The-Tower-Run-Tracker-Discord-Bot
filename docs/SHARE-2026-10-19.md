# Share history verification and overlap - 2026-10-19

Current lifecycle update: docs/SHARE-INVOCATIONS.md supersedes this release's no-completed-history-reuse policy only for explicitly bound, short-lived invocation snapshots. The verified reader, fresh submission checks and legacy Share fallback retain their safeguards; prepared public results identify their capture time.

## Failure evidence and bounded recovery

The October 18 20:22:19 UTC Share failure, correlation `868c10ca-a76a-4a52-93f9-8f99040e4c9e`, reached the main-page collected-count guard after 1,860 ms with no lock wait. That guard rejects either an empty page before the reported total or records exceeding that total. The historical privacy-safe diagnostic cannot distinguish these cases or establish their upstream cause. It does not identify the older startup issue or the separate repeated-record guard.

Each of these two main-page mismatch reasons now permits one complete fresh retry. Both attempts share the original 45-second deadline. A failed owner wave drains before retry; the second attempt starts without the first attempt's cursor or records. Cancellation prevents retry. A second mismatch retains its pagination classification and fails closed. Schema, ownership, repeated-record and extended-page failures do not gain retries.

Registered diagnostics include only an allowlisted reason and bounded numeric page index, returned count, collected count and expected total. They contain no document IDs, owners, payloads or response text. Metadata copies do not expose mutable registered state. Complete validated observations alone enter the durable verified-history snapshot.

## Independent reads and caller policy

Standard Share first reads the authoritative local cloud preference. Only then does it overlap fresh tracker settings, shared display settings and the existing locked history operation. Publication still waits for every input. Disabled-cloud callers retain local settings/history and never start these optional cloud reads; unreadable local preference storage fails before remote work or publication.

Concurrent unresolved provider identity reads for the same Discord owner share one underlying request. Each caller separately retains its optional, fail-on-lookup-error or required-linked policy. Failed lookups are evicted rather than cached as an unlinked account. Different owners remain separate, explicit refresh starts a new lookup, and existing completed-identity cache lifetimes are unchanged.

Completed run histories are not cached across actions. Review, submission and Share still obtain their own fresh cloud observations, including current website type/metric edits, deletions, count checks and extended data. An availability ping or unchanged count cannot validate old comparison data. Local acceptance, captured upload targets, pending-intent overlays, deletion tombstones, immutable share versions, cloud permission checks and protected historical queue decisions are unchanged.

## Timing evidence and limits

Before repair, 51 natural completed shares measured median 4.733 seconds, p95 9.381 seconds and maximum 10.036 seconds. A read-only 1,361-run probe took 7.508 seconds for six main/count requests plus fourteen extended batches; nine extended batches were empty. Identity lookup separately took 0.602 seconds. A 270-run probe took 2.760 seconds plus 0.667 seconds for identity. These are pre-repair production/backend observations, not postdeployment gains.

The isolated overlap regression uses 1,500 ms history and 500/2,000 ms tracker settings. Both inputs finish in their maximum rather than their sum, with no publication before completion. These are controlled fixture timings with mocked Discord delivery. Substantial history network cost remains; no 1-2-second production guarantee is made and extended data has not been dropped.
